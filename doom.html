Training uses the same primitive actions. Reward comes only from safely attributable consequences available in the current bridge—player health/death, survival cost, and a small first-visit spatial novelty bonus—not rules such as “enemy visible → fire.” Hostile HP loss and Chocolate Doom’s single-player intermission kill count remain visible telemetry but are not rewarded because the borrowed bridge cannot identify the attacker. Consequences are assigned with a 4-step discounted return so delayed events are credited across a short action sequence rather than only to the final tick.
